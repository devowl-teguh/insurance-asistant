# Cedar Home Policy (Fictional)

## Fire damage
This fictional home policy covers sudden accidental fire damage to the insured building up to $200,000, subject to a $500 deductible. The insured must take reasonable steps to prevent further damage and report the loss promptly.

## Flood exclusion
The home policy excludes damage caused by flood, surface water, and storm surge. A separate flood endorsement would be needed for flood coverage.

## Personal belongings
The home policy covers personal belongings damaged by a covered fire up to $25,000. Cash and business inventory are excluded.
