# Horizon Travel Policy (Fictional)

## Emergency medical treatment overseas
This fictional travel policy covers emergency medical treatment overseas up to $50,000 per insured person. Emergency treatment means medically necessary care for an unexpected illness or injury during the trip. The insured must contact the assistance service as soon as reasonably possible.

## Medical exclusions
The travel policy does not cover treatment that was planned before departure or routine medical checkups. Treatment arising from a pre-existing condition is excluded unless specifically listed as covered in the policy schedule.

## Trip cancellation
The travel policy reimburses non-refundable trip costs up to $3,000 if the insured cancels before departure because of a sudden serious illness, injury, or death of an immediate family member. The policy does not cover a change of mind.

## Baggage loss
The travel policy covers lost or stolen checked baggage up to $1,500 per trip, subject to a $100 deductible. Cash, passports, and unattended valuables are excluded.
